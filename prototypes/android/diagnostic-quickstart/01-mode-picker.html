Idea 1 · Choose before creating

Mode picker sheet

The Home CTA opens a focused bottom sheet. The user chooses what kind of help they want, and only then is the right AI session created.

  1. Choose a bike on Home.
  2. Tap Start a session.
  3. Try each help mode in the sheet.
  4. Use Back or Reset to compare paths.

Prototype seam: session creation, bike creation, and AI replies are simulated in memory.
