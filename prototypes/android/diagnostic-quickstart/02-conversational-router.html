Idea 2 · Let the conversation route

Conversational router

The Home CTA jumps straight into a neutral AI session. Bike Doc asks the first routing question in chat, so future help modes become part of the conversation.

  1. Choose a bike on Home.
  2. Tap Talk to Bike Doc.
  3. Choose any suggested intent.
  4. Notice how the session title and next prompt adapt.

Prototype seam: no server session exists until an intent is chosen; replies and composer behavior are simulated.
