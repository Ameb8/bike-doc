Idea 3 · Give the AI a useful head start

Home quick compose

The Home CTA expands into a compact launcher. The user chooses a mode and optionally gives one line of context before entering the session.

  1. Choose a bike and tap Start with Bike Doc.
  2. Switch between the three modes.
  3. Edit the optional starting detail.
  4. Begin and inspect the pre-seeded chat.

Prototype seam: the initial text is passed to a simulated session; photos, persistence, and AI replies are not connected.
